D&D Card Table v0.21.1 — Localization Coverage Hotfix

- Builder Data explanatory paragraph is translated.
- Counted navigation tabs now translate: Monsters · 331, Subclasses · 125, etc.
- Empty/fallback content card summary is translated.

- Common creature type labels shown as UI metadata are translated (Dragon, Aberration, Beast, Undead, etc.).
