D&D Card Table v0.18 — Class Choice Engine

No database migration is required. The existing `feature_choices` JSON state
and v0.17 always-prepared spell provenance are used.

NEW: CLASS CHOICES SECTION
Character -> Features now has a dedicated Class Choices section. Choices persist
on the character and are not just display notes.

CLERIC — DIVINE ORDER
Level 1:
- Protector
  Martial weapon proficiency + Heavy armor training are recorded/displayed.
- Thaumaturge
  +1 Cleric cantrip capacity.
  Arcana and Religion checks add Wisdom modifier (minimum +1).

DRUID — PRIMAL ORDER
Level 1:
- Magician
  +1 Druid cantrip capacity.
  Arcana and Nature checks add Wisdom modifier (minimum +1).
- Warden
  Martial weapon proficiency + Medium armor training are recorded/displayed.

WIZARD — SCHOLAR
Level 2:
- choose one proficient Arcana, History, Investigation, Medicine, Nature, or
  Religion skill.
- The character sheet treats that skill as Expertise (double PB).

SORCERER — METAMAGIC
- Level 2: 2 options
- Level 10: 4 options
- Level 17: 6 options
All ten SRD 5.2 options are available:
Careful, Distant, Empowered, Extended, Heightened, Quickened, Seeking,
Subtle, Transmuted, Twinned.
Each selected option has a Use button that spends the appropriate number of
Sorcery Points. The sheet also now has an Innate Sorcery 2-use resource.

WARLOCK — ELDRITCH INVOCATIONS
Invocation limits now follow the 1–20 Warlock table:
1 / 3 / 3 / 3 / 5 / 5 / 6 / 6 / 7 / 7 / 7 / 8 / 8 / 8 / 9 / 9 / 9 / 10 / 10 / 10

The Basic Rules invocation list is selectable in Features.
Validation includes:
- Warlock level prerequisites
- invocation-to-invocation prerequisites such as:
  Pact of the Blade -> Thirsting Blade -> Devouring Blade
  Pact of the Tome -> Gift of the Protectors
  Pact of the Chain -> Investment of the Chain Master
- targeted repeatable invocations only offer eligible currently-known Warlock
  cantrips or eligible Origin feats.
- a prerequisite invocation cannot be removed while a dependent invocation is
  selected.
Warlock level 2 also gains an auto-managed Magical Cunning resource.

SUBCLASS CHOICES
Circle of the Land:
- choose Arid / Polar / Temperate / Tropical.
- current land automatically controls the correct always-prepared Circle Spells.
- changing land updates the spell package.

Draconic Sorcery:
- at level 6 choose Acid / Cold / Fire / Lightning / Poison for Elemental Affinity.

College of Lore:
- at level 6 choose two Magical Discoveries from eligible Cleric, Druid, or
  Wizard spells.
- selected spells are always prepared and do not consume normal Bard prepared
  spell allowance.

SUBCLASS ALWAYS-PREPARED SPELLS
Automatically synchronized by level for:
- Life Domain
- Oath of Devotion
- Fiend Patron
- Draconic Sorcery
- Circle of the Land (depends on selected land)
- College of Lore Magical Discoveries (depends on user choice)

They:
- appear as Always Prepared in the Spells tab
- don't count against normal Prepared Spells
- are protected from accidental deletion
- update automatically after level changes / rollback / subclass-choice changes

LEVEL-UP
The guided Level Up screen now announces newly unlocked modeled choices such as:
- Metamagic increases
- Eldritch Invocation increases
- Wizard Scholar
- Circle land
- Draconic Elemental Affinity
- College of Lore Magical Discoveries

PENDING CHOICES
A small chip appears next to the character identity when the sheet has unresolved
modeled choices.

INSTALL
1. Stop server:
   Ctrl + C
2. Copy this update into dnd-card-table with replacement.
3. No migration is required.
4. Run:
   npm.cmd run web
5. Ctrl + F5.
