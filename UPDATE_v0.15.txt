D&D Card Table v0.15 — Character Progression & Core Feature Choices

This stage starts turning normalized class features into character-state mechanics.

NEW: PROGRESSION
- Character -> Features now contains a Progression panel.
- Level can be changed from 1 to 20.
- HP Maximum is intentionally entered by the user (fixed/rolled HP remains your choice).
- Proficiency Bonus updates automatically.
- Class resources and spell-slot tables resynchronize on reload.
- Subclass can be selected from the imported SRD subclass belonging to the character's class.
- Dropping below level 3 removes the subclass.
- If a level reduction lowers a choice limit, Weapon Mastery / Expertise selections are safely trimmed.

NEW: WEAPON MASTERY
- Supported classes: Barbarian, Fighter, Paladin, Ranger, Rogue.
- Limits follow the SRD 5.2.1 class tables:
  Barbarian 2 -> 3 at 4 -> 4 at 10
  Fighter 3 -> 4 at 4 -> 5 at 10 -> 6 at 16
  Paladin/Ranger/Rogue: 2
- Weapon eligibility is class-aware.
- Rogue martial mastery requires Finesse or Light.
- Barbarian mastery is limited to Simple/Martial Melee weapons.
- The selection list is powered by the audited local equipment library.
- Mastered weapons get a Mastery badge and a short rules reminder.
- Encounter actions now show equipped weapons only, and display mastered properties.

NEW: EXPERTISE
- Rogue: 2 at level 1, 4 at level 6.
- Bard: 2 at level 2, 4 at level 9.
- Ranger: 1 at level 2, 3 at level 9.
- Only already-proficient skills can receive Expertise.
- Skill modifiers automatically use double Proficiency Bonus.
- Expertise is visually marked in the skill list.

NEW: FIGHTING STYLE
- Fighter level 1; Paladin/Ranger level 2.
- Choices come from normalized SRD Fighting Style feats in Builder Data.
- Defense is mechanically connected to Auto AC (+1 while wearing armor).
- Archery is mechanically connected to attacks with Ranged weapons (+2).
- Great Weapon Fighting and Two-Weapon Fighting are recorded and surfaced,
  but their attack-sequence/die-floor behavior is not automated yet.

FEATURE NAVIGATION
- Unlocked class/subclass/species/feat cards now link back to their Builder Data source entry.

INSTALL
1. Make sure v0.14 has been installed and the item audit has been run.
2. Stop the web server: Ctrl + C
3. Copy this update into dnd-card-table with replacement.
4. Run:
   npm.cmd run db:migrate:015
5. Run:
   npm.cmd run web
6. Browser: Ctrl + F5
7. Open a character -> Features.

GOOD TEST CHARACTERS
- Fighter: Fighting Style + 3 masteries at level 1.
- Barbarian: 2 masteries at level 1; 3 at level 4.
- Rogue: 2 masteries + 2 Expertise at level 1.
- Bard: Expertise appears at level 2.
- Ranger: 2 masteries; Expertise + Fighting Style appear at level 2.
- Paladin: 2 masteries at level 1; Fighting Style appears at level 2.
